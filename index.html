HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently asked questions.

Explore answers about the Orbis Decision Intelligence Platform and its six enterprise management modules.

What is Orbis?

Orbis is a Decision Intelligence Platform that brings six enterprise management capabilities together: Discovery Management, Portfolio Management, Vendor Management, Project Management, Development Hub, and IT Financial Management.

What are the six core modules of Orbis?

The six modules are Discovery Management, Portfolio Management, Vendor Management, Project Management, Development Hub, and IT Financial Management. Each addresses a different area of enterprise management.

Who can use Orbis?

Orbis is intended for enterprise stakeholders across executive leadership, portfolio management, procurement, project management, IT, development, and finance.

How does Orbis support decision-making?

Orbis brings business and operational management areas together, helping organizations gain a broader view of initiatives, investments, vendors, projects, development activities, and IT financial resources.

Can Orbis support multiple business functions?

Yes. Orbis is structured around six connected modules designed to address different enterprise management functions within one platform.

How does Orbis support portfolio management?

Its Portfolio Management module focuses on managing strategic portfolios, initiatives, investment priorities, and their alignment with organizational objectives.

Does Orbis support vendor management?

Orbis includes a Vendor Management module for organizing vendor information and supporting vendor relationship oversight.

How does Orbis help manage projects?

The Project Management module supports project planning, coordination, progress monitoring, and execution oversight.

What is the purpose of the Development Hub?

Development Hub provides a dedicated management area for organizing and overseeing development activities and related workflows.

Does Orbis include IT Financial Management?

Yes. IT Financial Management is one of the six core modules and focuses on IT budgets, expenditure, and technology-related financial resources.

Can Orbis integrate with existing enterprise systems?

Integration requirements and supported systems depend on Orbis's actual technical capabilities and configuration. Contact the Orbis team to discuss your organization's integration requirements.

How can we learn more about Orbis?

You can request a product demonstration to explore the platform, understand its modules, and discuss how it may align with your organization's requirements.
